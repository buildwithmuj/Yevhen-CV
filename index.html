Profile

Senior Frontend Engineer with a decade of building for the web. As a member of Scrum and Kanban teams I've developed a variety of products: corporate websites, a clinical dental notes application, a public food delivery e-commerce platform, and several companies' in-house CMS solutions.

I've worked in both strictly regulated environments and fast-moving companies, responsible for taking features from implementation to production and making sure they delivered a great customer experience. Along the way I've learned from great mentors and mentored junior developers myself, as I genuinely enjoy sharing knowledge and helping others grow.

AI-assisted development is now part of my daily workflow, with tools such as Cursor and Claude Code helping me automate repetitive tasks, explore ideas and designs faster, and accelerate software delivery.
